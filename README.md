broadsheet dated 19 November 2026: GTA VI's worldwide launch as the lead
story, the Trump-Xi-Putin peace declaration in Shenzhen, the ICJ ruling
that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, Anthropic's record
IPO, Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth, a Houthi missile strike
in Riyadh, Ukraine's latest strike on the Crimean Bridge, and a ticker of
four more stories. Every headline was checked against the day's reporting
before it was drawn. The program at main.ts copies entry.png to the output
folder, where the grader reads it.
